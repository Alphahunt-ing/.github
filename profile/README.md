**AlphaHunt is a private crypto intel circle. Spot it early. Act on it every day.**
Members post links; each one gets a clear verdict (act, watch or skip), every idea and trade is
logged, and everyone keeps their own keys and signs their own trades.

- **Read and score.** Claude finds the token and chain, pulls live stats, scores a six-point
  checklist, and flags paid posts and referral links.
- **Validate.** Grok agents check X for real attention versus paid shills and bot-like hype. If the
  two disagree, a human looks.
- **Log everything.** Ideas, trades, airdrops and payouts, so members learn from their own data.
- **Contributor rewards.** Whoever posts the call shares in the profit members make on it.
- **Your keys.** Nobody pools money. Vault, trading and burner wallets; the app stores public
  addresses only.
